# Picking the dates

Two variables: **how long**, and **which moon**. They interact, which is why this needs
its own page.

The bio bay is the only thing on the trip that cares about the date.
**[[bio-bays|All three bays]]** need a dark night. Everything else works any week.

---

## If it's a week — [[week-long|full page]]

A week only needs **one** dark night, so the moon mostly stops mattering. Mostly.

| Week | Darkest night | Ends on |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon Feb 8 → Mon Feb 15** ⭐ | **6%**, then 12% the next night | **Presidents' Day** |
| Mon Jan 11 → Mon Jan 18 | 16%, degrading all week | MLK Day |
| Mon Jan 18 → Mon Jan 25 | **84–100% every night** | — |

**Feb 8–15 wins outright.** Two dark nights plus a spare, ends on a federal holiday so
it's six days off rather than seven, and it's peak humpback season.

**The MLK week is the worst week of the quarter for this.** Full moon lands on Jan 22.

---

## If it stays a long weekend

Moon illumination on the Saturday night of each Fri–Mon weekend:

| Weekend | Moon | | Holiday |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan 8–11 | 4% | ★ excellent | no |
| **Jan 15–18** | **66%** | poor | ✅ **MLK** |
| Jan 22–25 | 99% | bad | no |
| Jan 29–Feb 1 | 42% | ok | no |
| **Feb 5–8** | **0%** | ★ perfect | no |
| **Feb 12–15** | **50%** | ok | ✅ **Presidents'** |
| Feb 19–22 | 100% | bad | no |
| Mar 5–8 | 2% | ★ excellent | no |

**You can have the holiday Monday or a dark bay, not both.**

- **Keep MLK** — 66% moon, dim bay, but nobody burns a vacation day
- **Presidents' Day** — 50%, still a three-day weekend, drier month, whales in. **Best
  compromise**
- **Feb 5–8** — a true new moon and a perfect bay, but it's an ordinary weekend so
  everyone takes the Monday off

---

## The question that decides it

**Is the bio bay a must-do, or a nice-to-have?**

- **Must-do + a week** → Feb 8–15
- **Must-do + a weekend** → Feb 5–8
- **Nice-to-have** → keep MLK, or Presidents' Day if February suits people better

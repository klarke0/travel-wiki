# Laguna Grande — Fajardo

The **accessible** bio bay. Inside the **Reserva Natural Cabezas de San Juan** on the
northeast corner, about **an hour's drive from San Juan**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Brightness** | Middle of the three. Dimmer than [[mosquito-bay\|Mosquito Bay]], brighter than [[la-parguera\|La Parguera]] |
| **Swimming** | ❌ Not permitted |
| **From San Juan** | ~1 hour, no ferry |
| **Format** | Kayak, usually through a mangrove channel to the lagoon |

**Why it's worth considering over Vieques:** it turns the bio bay from a full day plus a
ferry into an evening trip. If the weekend is tight, or if the group doesn't want to
commit a whole day to it, this is the pragmatic pick.

**Why it isn't the default:** it's noticeably dimmer, and the paddle out through the
mangroves is part of the tour rather than a bonus. On a bright-moon night the difference
between this and Mosquito Bay matters more, not less.

Pairs naturally with [[fajardo|Fajardo]] and [[luquillo|Luquillo]] in one day.

# Guavate — the Pork Highway

A road lined with **lechoneras**, roadside spit-roast pork joints, about 45 minutes south
of San Juan in the mountains.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Half day |
| **Cost** | Cheap |
| **Best** | Weekends, when it's busy and the music is on |

Order lechón by the pound. **The most Puerto Rican thing on this list** and the one
people tend to remember.

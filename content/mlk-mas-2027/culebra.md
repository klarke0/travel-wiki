# Culebra

Small island off the east coast, seven miles long, best known for one beach.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Full day, whichever way you go |
| **Getting there** | **Ferry from Ceiba** — cheap and unreliable. **Short flight** — neither |

## Things to do

**Flamenco Beach** — regularly named one of the best beaches in the world. A mile-long
crescent of white sand in a protected horseshoe bay, so the water is calm and clear.

**The tanks on Flamenco.** Two rusting US Marine Corps tanks sit half-buried at the north
end of the beach, left over from when the island was a bombing range. They're covered in
graffiti now and are the island's oddest landmark.

**Playa Zoni** — on the far east end, longer drive, far fewer people. Views across to
[[vieques|Vieques]] and St Thomas on a clear day.

**Playa Tamarindo** — the snorkelling one. Sea turtles are common, reef close to shore.

**Culebrita** — an uninhabited islet off the east coast with a ruined lighthouse and tide
pools. Boat trip from the main island.

## The catch

A full day committed either way, so this competes directly with [[el-yunque|El Yunque]]
and with a [[mosquito-bay|bio bay]] night on [[vieques|Vieques]]. **On a three-night trip
you realistically get one of the three.**

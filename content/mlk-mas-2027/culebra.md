# Culebra

Small island off the east coast, and home to **Flamenco Beach**, regularly named one of
the best beaches in the world.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Full day, whichever way you go |
| **Getting there** | **Ferry from Ceiba** — cheap and unreliable. **Short flight** — neither |

A full day committed either way, so it competes directly with [[el-yunque|El Yunque]] and
with a [[mosquito-bay|bio bay]] night on [[vieques|Vieques]]. On a three-night trip you
realistically get one of the three.

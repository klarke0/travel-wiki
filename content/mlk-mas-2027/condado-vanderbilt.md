# Condado Vanderbilt

**The best balance of nice and convenient.** A 1919 landmark on the beach in Condado, San
Juan, and you can walk out to dinner.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Config for 4** | **Two rooms** — around **\$600–1,000/night** for the group |
| **Ballpark** | **\$300–500 / room**, averaging about \$415 |
| **From SJU** | ~15 min |
| **Link** | [condadovanderbilt.com](https://www.condadovanderbilt.com/) |

Comparable in total to a villa, but with a beach, bars, restaurants and nobody doing
washing up.

**The catch:** two rooms means no shared living room, which matters if MLK-mas involves a
morning together with presents. If that's the point of the trip, look at
[[palmas-del-mar|a villa]] or the [[dorado-beach|Dorado residences]] instead.

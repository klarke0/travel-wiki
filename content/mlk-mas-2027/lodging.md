# Lodging — 4 people

Everything here sleeps **four**. Prices are **2026 ballparks** to show the tiers, not
quotes. **January and February are peak season, so assume the top of each range.**

## The decision that actually matters

**One place with a kitchen, or two hotel rooms?** If MLK-mas means presents and a morning
together in pyjamas, you need a living room and a kitchen, and that rules out the hotels.

## Rough read

- **Best value:** [[palmas-del-mar|Palmas del Mar]]. Villa, kitchen, pool, under what two
  hotel rooms cost
- **Best balance:** [[condado-vanderbilt|Condado Vanderbilt]]. Walkable, on a beach
- **Best splurge:** [[dorado-beach|Dorado Beach]]
- **Most characterful:** [[el-convento|El Convento]], if nobody needs a beach

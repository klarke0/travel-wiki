# Vieques

![Vieques from the air](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5f/Vieques_from_air.jpg/1280px-Vieques_from_air.jpg)
*Vieques. Photo: US Fish and Wildlife Service, public domain*

Island off the east coast, about 21 miles long. Quiet, low-key, **wild horses on the
roads**, and far less developed than the main island. Two thirds of it is a National
Wildlife Refuge, which is why it stayed that way.

Reached by **ferry from Ceiba** ([[fajardo|Fajardo]] area) or a short flight.

## Things to do

**[[mosquito-bay|Mosquito Bay]]** — the brightest bioluminescent bay in the world, and the
main reason to come.

**Sun Bay (Sombé)** — the big public balneario, a long crescent of sand, usually calm.

**Playa Negra** — a **black sand beach**, volcanic, reached by a short walk up a
streambed. Genuinely unusual and worth the effort.

**Playa Caracas and Playa La Chiva** — inside the wildlife refuge, on the old naval land.
Some of the least developed beaches you'll find anywhere this accessible.

**The wild horses.** They roam freely across the island and you'll see them on the roads
and beaches. Drive carefully at night, especially near Esperanza.

**Esperanza** — the small south-coast town where the bio bay tours stage. The malecón,
a handful of bars and restaurants, and the whole thing walkable in ten minutes.

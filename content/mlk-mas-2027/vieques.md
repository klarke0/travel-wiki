# Vieques

![Vieques from the air](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5f/Vieques_from_air.jpg/1280px-Vieques_from_air.jpg)
*Vieques. Photo: US Fish and Wildlife Service, public domain*

Island off Puerto Rico's east coast, about 21 miles long. Home to
**[[mosquito-bay|Mosquito Bay]]**, which is the reason it's on this list. Quiet, low-key,
wild horses on the roads, and far less developed than the main island.

Reached by **ferry from Ceiba** (cheap, unreliable) or a short flight (neither).

# Puerto Rico

**January is Puerto Rico's best month.** About 82°F in the day, 72°F at night, sea around
81°F, one of the driest months. No passport, US phone plans work, US dollars.

### Two pairings that work

- **[[el-yunque|El Yunque]] in the morning, [[luquillo|Luquillo]] kiosks in the
  afternoon.** Fifteen minutes apart, and the classic day out here.
- **[[fajardo|Fajardo]] beach by day, [[laguna-grande|Laguna Grande]] bio bay after
  dark.** Same corner of the island, no ferry.

### And one that doesn't

**[[mosquito-bay|Mosquito Bay]], [[culebra|Culebra]] and [[el-yunque|El Yunque]] each eat
most of a day and pull in different directions.** On three nights you get one or two, not
all three. Pick early.

## Where to base

Puerto Rico is small but the drive times are real. Pick one base.

| Base | From SJU | What it gets you |
|---|---|---|
| **Condado / [[old-san-juan\|Old San Juan]]** | 10–20 min | Walkable, restaurants, forts. Least car-dependent |
| **Isla Verde** | 5–10 min | Beach, closest to the airport, less charm |
| **Río Grande / Bahía Beach** | ~30 min | Resort quiet, [[el-yunque\|El Yunque]] next door |
| **Dorado** | ~40 min | The high end. Nothing to walk to |
| **[[rincon\|Rincón]]** (west) | ~2h15m | Whales. Too far to day-trip comfortably |

For three nights with a group, **Condado or Old San Juan** is the sane default.

# Fajardo

The northeast corner. **The jumping-off point for everything east**: ferries, the
accessible bio bay, and the best family beach on that coast.

## Things to do

**[[laguna-grande|Laguna Grande]]** — the bio bay inside the Cabezas de San Juan reserve.
An hour from San Juan, no ferry needed.

**Seven Seas Beach** — a protected cove with calm water, the most family-friendly beach
in Fajardo. Shade under the palms, views up to the lighthouse on the reserve. **Decent
snorkelling**, and if you walk about half a mile northeast off Hwy 987 to what locals call
Hidden Beach, the reefs are just offshore.

**Reserva Natural Cabezas de San Juan** — the protected headland with the lighthouse,
mangroves and the lagoon.

**The ferry terminal at Ceiba**, just south, is how you reach [[vieques|Vieques]] and
[[culebra|Culebra]]. Cheap and unreliable, so build slack around it.

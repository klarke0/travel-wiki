# Puerto Rico's three bioluminescent bays

**Puerto Rico has three, not one**, and they are not interchangeable. Picking the wrong
one for what you actually want is the easiest mistake here.

| | Brightness | Swim? | From San Juan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[mosquito-bay\|Mosquito Bay]]**, Vieques | ★★★ **Brightest in the world** | ❌ No | Ferry + taxi, or a flight |
| **[[laguna-grande\|Laguna Grande]]**, Fajardo | ★★ Middle | ❌ No | **~1 hour drive** |
| **[[la-parguera\|La Parguera]]**, Lajas | ★ Dimmest | ✅ **Yes** | ~3 hour drive |

## How to choose

**Want the best possible glow →** [[mosquito-bay|Mosquito Bay]]. It holds the Guinness
record and nothing else is close. Costs you most of a day and a ferry.

**Want to actually get in the water →** [[la-parguera|La Parguera]]. **It's the only one
of the three where swimming is allowed**, and swimming in it is a genuinely different
experience from watching it from a kayak: your arms trail blue light, your hands glow as
you pull them through. It's the dimmest bay, so it's a straight trade of intensity for
immersion. Three hours each way is the price.

**Want it to be easy →** [[laguna-grande|Laguna Grande]]. An hour from San Juan, doable
as an evening out rather than a whole day, and brighter than La Parguera.

> **All three depend on a dark night.** The moon matters more than which bay you pick.

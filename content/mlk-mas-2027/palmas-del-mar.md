# Palmas del Mar villas

**The value play.** A large residential resort community in Humacao on the east coast,
with a lot of privately let villas.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Config for 4** | **2–3 bedroom villa**, kitchen, often a private pool |
| **Ballpark** | **\$300–600 / night** for the whole place |
| **Per person** | ~\$75–150 pp/night for four |
| **From SJU** | ~1 hour |
| **Link** | [Vrbo listings](https://www.vrbo.com/vacation-rentals/puerto-rico/palmas-del-mar) |

**Cheaper than two hotel rooms, and you get a kitchen and a living room.** For a trip
that's explicitly a family Christmas, that combination is hard to argue with.

**The catch:** you need a car, there's nothing walkable, and quality varies listing to
listing in a way a hotel's doesn't. Read reviews properly.

Also the closest base to the [[vieques|Vieques]] ferry at Ceiba, which matters if
[[mosquito-bay|the bio bay]] is happening.

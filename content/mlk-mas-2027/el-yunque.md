# El Yunque National Forest

**The only tropical rainforest in the US Forest System.** About 45 minutes from San Juan.
Waterfalls, short trails, swimming holes.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Half to full day |
| **Cost** | Free, or a timed-entry fee — unclear, see below |
| **Drive** | ~45 min from San Juan. **You need a car** |

## ⚠️ Verify the entry rules before you go

Recreation.gov has required a **timed reservation** for the PR-191 corridor, booked in
advance. The Forest Service has separately published that the corridor is free with no
reservation needed. **The two contradict each other right now.**

Check close to the date. Turning up without a reservation when one is required means
being turned around at the gate, and it's a 45-minute drive each way.

[recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/facility/300017) ·
[Forest Service conditions](https://fs.usda.gov/r08/elyunque/conditions)

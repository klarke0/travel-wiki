# Hotel El Convento

A **350-year-old Carmelite convent** in the middle of [[old-san-juan|Old San Juan]].

| | |
|---|---|
| **Config for 4** | Two rooms |
| **Ballpark** | **\$250–400 / room** |
| **From SJU** | ~20 min |
| **Link** | [elconvento.com](https://elconvento.com/) |

**There is no beach.** That is the entire point. You stay here because you want to be
inside the old city, walk out into it at night, and come back to a courtyard that has
been there since the 1600s.

The right pick if the trip is about the place rather than the pool. The wrong pick if
anyone in the group is expecting to sit on sand outside their room.

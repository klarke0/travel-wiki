# Old San Juan

The 500-year-old walled city. **The one thing on this trip everybody will enjoy**, and it
needs no car, no reservation and no planning.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Half day, easily a full one |
| **Cost** | Small National Park Service fee for the forts |
| **Getting there** | Walkable if you stay here or in Condado |

## What's there

- **Castillo San Felipe del Morro (El Morro)** — the big seaward fort on the point
- **Castillo San Cristóbal** — the larger land-side fort, usually quieter
- **Paseo de la Princesa** — the promenade along the city wall
- The blue cobblestones, the painted houses, and a serious amount of good food

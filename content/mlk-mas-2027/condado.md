# Condado & Isla Verde

The two beach neighbourhoods of San Juan proper. **This is where you stay if you want a
beach and a walkable dinner in the same trip.**

## Condado

The upmarket strip, 10–15 minutes from the airport and walking distance to
[[old-san-juan|Old San Juan]] along the water if you're willing.

- **Condado Beach** — city beach, decent, right there
- **Ashford Avenue** — the main drag, restaurants and bars end to end
- **La Ventana al Mar** — the open park on the seafront, good for a sunset with a drink
- **Laguna del Condado** — the lagoon on the inland side. **Paddleboarding and kayaking**,
  calm water, manatees turn up

Base here and you can do the old city, the beach and dinner without driving.
See [[condado-vanderbilt|Condado Vanderbilt]].

## Isla Verde

Five to ten minutes from the airport, so it's the easy landing spot.

- **Isla Verde Beach** — wide, sandy, generally considered the better beach of the two
- Less charm than Condado, more resort

**Good for the first or last night** given the airport proximity, particularly with a
group arriving on three different schedules.

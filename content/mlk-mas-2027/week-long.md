# If it's a week, not a weekend

Everyone's flying a long way, so a week may make more sense than three nights. **It
changes the trip completely**, and it changes the answer to the date question.

---

## 🌑 A week fixes the bio bay problem — but only if it's the right week

A weekend has to gamble on one night's moon. **A week just needs one dark night in it**,
and you schedule the bay around it.

But that only works if the week sits near a new moon. Anchoring a week on MLK does not
rescue it.

### Option A — **Mon Feb 8 → Mon Feb 15** ⭐

**Ends on Presidents' Day**, so it's six days off work, not seven.

| Night | Moon | |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon Feb 8** | **6%** | ★ bio bay night |
| **Tue Feb 9** | **12%** | ★ backup bio bay night |
| Wed Feb 10 | 20% | still fine |
| Thu Feb 11 | 29% | |
| Fri Feb 12 | 39% | |
| Sat Feb 13 | 50% | |
| Sun Feb 14 | 60% | |
| Mon Feb 15 | 70% | Presidents' Day, fly home |

**Two genuinely dark nights, plus a spare.** That matters, because a cloudy night or a
cancelled tour doesn't kill the plan. It's also **peak humpback season** off
[[rincon|Rincón]].

### Option B — Mon Jan 11 → Mon Jan 18, ending on MLK

Best night is **Jan 11 at 16%**, and it gets worse every night after. Workable, not good.

### Option C — the MLK week itself, Jan 18–25

**Don't.** The moon runs **84% to 100%** across that entire week. Full moon is Jan 22.
There is no dark night in it at all.

> **The short version: if the bio bay matters and it's a week, go Feb 8–15.**

---

## What a week unlocks that a weekend doesn't

On three nights, [[mosquito-bay|Mosquito Bay]], [[culebra|Culebra]] and
[[el-yunque|El Yunque]] compete for the same days. **On seven, they don't.**

- **All three [[bio-bays|bio bays]] become reachable**, including
  [[la-parguera|La Parguera]] in the southwest, the only one you can swim in. Three hours
  each way is fine inside a week; it isn't inside a weekend
- **[[rincon|Rincón]] and the west coast** stop being a write-off. Whales, surf, and it's
  on the way to La Parguera
- **[[vieques|Vieques]] as a two-night stay** rather than a rushed day trip
- Actual downtime, which matters when half the group has just done a 10-hour travel day

---

## A shape that works

Not a schedule, a shape. Two bases, one move.

**Days 1–4, east: base in [[condado|Condado]] or [[old-san-juan|Old San Juan]]**
- Arrival day. Nothing planned, everyone lands at different times
- [[old-san-juan\|Old San Juan]] — forts, the walled city, dinner out
- [[el-yunque\|El Yunque]] in the morning, [[luquillo\|Luquillo]] kiosks after
- [[fajardo\|Fajardo]] beach, then **[[laguna-grande\|Laguna Grande]] bio bay after dark**
  if the moon's right, or a day out to [[culebra\|Culebra]]

**Days 5–7, west: base near [[rincon|Rincón]]**
- Drive west, stop at [[guavate\|Guavate]] for lechón on the way
- **Whale watching** off Rincón
- **[[la-parguera\|La Parguera]]** — the swimmable bio bay, south of Rincón
- Back to San Juan for the flight

**Or skip the west and do [[vieques|Vieques]] for two nights instead**, with
[[mosquito-bay|Mosquito Bay]] on the darkest night. That's the version if the brightest
bay is the point and swimming in one isn't.

## What to re-check if it becomes a week

- **Lodging maths changes.** A villa gets much better value against seven nights of hotel
  rooms. See [[lodging|lodging]]
- **A car goes from optional to required**
- Two bases means two bookings, and the west coast is a different market entirely

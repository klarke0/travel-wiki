# Rincón — whale watching

Surf town on the **west** coast, about **2h15m** from San Juan.

## 🐋 Why it matters for this trip

**Humpback whales are off this coast January through March, peaking in February.** It's
genuinely seasonal, and it's one of the few things on this list you can't do most of the
year.

**This is an argument for the February dates.** If the trip moves to Presidents' Day
weekend, the whales are near peak. On MLK weekend they're in but early.

The distance is the catch. 2h15m each way makes it a committed day, or a reason to spend
a night on the west coast rather than day-tripping it.

# Luquillo

Coastal town on the northeast, right below [[el-yunque|El Yunque]]. **The classic pairing
is rainforest in the morning, beach and kiosks in the afternoon**, and it works because
they're fifteen minutes apart.

## 🍤 The Luquillo Kiosks

**More than 60 food kiosks in a row along the beach.** Fritters, pinchos, mofongo,
seafood stews, cold beer, fresh juice. This is one of the best-known eating strips on the
island and it's cheap.

**Local tip worth having:** most visitors stop at the first and most visible kiosks. The
better finds are **further down the strip**, where the family-run places do traditional
seafood, homemade pastries, and there's decent vegan Puerto Rican food too.

## The beaches

- **Balneario La Monserrate** — the main beach, calm, palm-lined, easy
- **La Pared** — a short walk from the main beach. **Surf break**, less crowded, good for
  a lesson or just watching

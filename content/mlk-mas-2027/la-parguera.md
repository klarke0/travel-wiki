# La Parguera — Lajas

**The only bio bay in Puerto Rico where you can swim.** Southwest coast, near Lajas,
about **three hours from San Juan**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Brightness** | Dimmest of the three |
| **Swimming** | ✅ **Yes — the only one** |
| **From San Juan** | ~3 hours |
| **Format** | Boat out, then in the water |

**This is the trade.** [[mosquito-bay|Mosquito Bay]] is far brighter but you watch it from
a kayak. Here it's dimmer, but you get in, and swimming through bioluminescence is a
different thing entirely: light trailing off your arms, your hands glowing as they move.

**Three hours each way makes it a commitment**, realistically an overnight rather than a
day trip. On a three-night weekend that's probably too much. Worth knowing it exists,
and worth a proper look if the trip ever stretches to a week.

# Long Weekend Routes

Everything here fits in **1 to 3 days**. Washington first, because that's the biggest
gap in the catalogue, then eastern Oregon.

Distances, climbing, surface splits and GPX links are pulled straight from the route
guides. Drive times are from Portland.

Researched 2026-09-01.

---

## Washington — 2 to 3 days

Seven routes, all weekend-sized. **Southern Washington is closer to you than most of the
Oregon catalogue is** — Gifford Pinchot is a two-hour drive.

Two worth calling out. **Olympic Adventure Route** is difficulty 3 with 75% singletrack,
which is an unusual combination — purpose-built, flowy, non-technical trail. Easiest
entry to real singletrack on this list. **Goat Mountain Gusto** is the opposite: 37 miles
but 100% singletrack and 8,460 ft, so it rides far longer than the distance suggests.

The **Fire & Ice Cave Loop** connects two lava-tube caves through 20,000-year-old lava
beds, huckleberry fields and subalpine meadow. Best oddity on the list.

## Palouse to Cascades Trail — pick a section

251 miles of rail trail across Washington, Cedar Falls near North Bend to the Idaho
border. Nobody sensible rides it all in one go, and you don't need to — **it's meant to
be sectioned**, and rail grade means a beginner can cover real distance.

- **Cedar Falls to the Snoqualmie Tunnel** — 18.8 mi, +1,904 ft, average grade **1.8%**.
  The tunnel is **2.3 miles long, the longest rail-trail tunnel in the United States**.
  Bring real lights, it's pitch black and cold. **Closed November 1 to May 1.**
- **Cedar Falls to the Columbia** — 111 mi, the classic western section, 2-3 days.
- **Central section** — 107 mi, tunnel east through Ellensburg, over the Columbia on the
  **reopened Beverly Bridge**, ending at the Royal City cut-off. That bridge being back
  is what makes through-riding work again.

Bike-in camping at **Alice Creek** (MP 2127.1) and **Carter Creek** (MP 2123.2) — three
or four sites each, tables and vault toilets, reachable only on foot or by bike.

[Trail info →](https://www.palousetocascadestrail.org/westofcolumbia)

## Eastern Oregon — 2 to 3 days

Eastern Oregon is where the long routes live, so short options are genuinely thin. These
are the ones that fit a weekend.

### Steens Mountain Loop — the pick

**72 mi · +7,300 ft · 85% gravel · starts and ends at the Frenchglen Hotel**

Climbs to **9,700 ft, the highest road in Oregon**, up the north side and down the
south. Summit runs about 15°F cooler than Frenchglen. Second half has almost no shade.

- **2-day**: camp at **South Steens, mile 43**
- **3-day**: camp at **Fish Lake (mi 16.5)** or **Jackman Park (mi 18.5)** on night one

**Season is the constraint. The loop road is generally open early July to November** and
closes progressively with snow. Check TripCheck before committing to the drive, and call
the Burns District BLM for conditions.

It's about 5.5 hours from Portland, so this is a three-day trip minimum on driving alone.

[Route guide →](https://dirtyfreehub.org/routes/oregon/steens/)

### Elkhorn Crest Trail

**52 mi · 2 days · +4,750 ft · 75% unpaved · 50% singletrack · difficulty 8**

Already in the [[index|main catalogue]]. Short but **difficulty 8** — the hardest
thing on this page by a wide margin. Baker City, about 5 hours out.
[Details →](https://bikepacking.com/routes/elkhorn-crest-trail/)

### Scenic Bikeways in the east

From [[more-oregon-routes|the bikeway list]], these are the eastern ones that work as an
overnight rather than a tour: **Sherar's Falls** 33 mi (Maupin, ~2h),
**Madras Mountain Views** 29 mi, **Crooked River Canyon** 18 mi (Prineville). Signed,
mapped, paved. Good if the point is riding somewhere new rather than riding rough.

### If a long weekend stretches to four or five days

- **Owyhees Tour** — 225 mi, +14,000 ft, 4 days, loop from Jordan Valley. 75% gravel.
  Silver City ghost town, Owyhee Uplands Backcountry Byway, Three Forks Hot Springs.
  **September is the window** — summer is mosquito hell, late September risks hard
  freezes. 40mm+ tyres. Day 2 is a 29-mile climb gaining 4,000 ft.
  [Guide →](https://dirtyfreehub.org/routes/oregon/owyhees-tours/)
- **Wallowas Tour** — 300 mi, +21,000 ft, 5 days from Baker City, and only 35% gravel.
  Sleeps in motels and lodges with showers every night rather than camping, which makes
  it a very different kind of trip. Crux is Day 3's Old Imnaha Road, no cell service.
  [Guide →](https://dirtyfreehub.org/routes/oregon/wallowas-tour/)

# More Oregon Routes

The [[index|main catalogue]] covers bikepacking.com's 18 Oregon routes. These come from
everywhere else: the state's own bikeway program, the Oregon Timber Trail's own tier
breakdown, the long rail trails, and the Adventure Cycling network.

Researched 2026-09-01.

---

## Oregon Scenic Bikeways

Oregon runs **the only state scenic bikeway program in the country**. 17 designated
routes, chosen and vetted by the state, **signed on the road**, with published maps and
GPS files. Mostly paved, which makes them a different animal from the gravel routes in
the main catalogue, but the signage and documentation are far better than anything else
on offer.

## Oregon Timber Trail — by tier

The catalogue lists the OTT as one 669-mile, 25-day undertaking. It isn't only that.
**It's built to be section-ridden**, and the four tiers are each a trip in their own
right. Over 70% singletrack across the whole thing.

| Tier | Miles | Climbing | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deschutes** | 130 | +10,000 ft | Central Oregon — smallest, best entry point |
| **Willamette** | 148 | +13,000 ft | Central Cascades |
| **Hood** | 193 | +24,000 ft | Mt Hood — **most climbing of the four** |
| **Fremont** | 201 | +19,000 ft | S Oregon, CA border — longest |

The [[routes/anaxshat-passage|Anaxshat Passage]] in the main catalogue is a 120-mile loop
version of the Hood Tier, and it's the gentler way in: 13% singletrack rather than 70%,
with road detours available.

[Tier detail →](https://oregontimbertrail.org/long-distance-bikepacking)

## Rail trails

Flat, non-technical, and the easiest surface in the state. Useful as trips in their own
right or as connectors.

| Trail | Miles | Surface | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OC&E Woods Line** | **100+** | old railbed | Klamath Falls to Bly and north to Sycan Marsh. **Oregon's longest rail trail.** Doubles as the first leg of the Oregon Outback |
| **Banks-Vernonia** | 21 | fully paved | NW of Portland. The state's first rails-to-trails |
| **Crown Zellerbach** | 17 | mostly packed gravel | Scappoose to Vernonia, reclaimed logging road |

**Banks-Vernonia and Crown Zellerbach connect**, which makes a loop with two campgrounds
on it, Scaponia Park and Vernonia Lake. Covered in more detail in
[[beginner-routes-near-portland]].

For the OC&E as a bikepacking trip, bikepacking.com's Oregon Outback route runs
Klamath Falls to creek camping along it, 68 miles on day one.

## Adventure Cycling network

Long-distance routes with published maps, services data and established support.

- **TransAmerica Trail** — the eastern end is Yorktown, Virginia. **The western end is
  Astoria, Oregon.** Section 2 covers the Oregon stretch, over 5,324 ft McKenzie Pass,
  with a lower Santiam Pass alternate when McKenzie is snowed in.
- **Pacific Coast Route** — runs the full Oregon coast, the classic Highway 101 tour.
- **Sierra Cascades** — enters Oregon from California, follows the Cascade spine north.

You can ride a section rather than the whole thing. Astoria to Eugene, or the coast in
chunks, are both normal ways to use these.

[Route network →](https://www.adventurecycling.org/routes-and-maps/adventure-cycling-route-network/)

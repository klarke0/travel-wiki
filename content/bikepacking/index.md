## Reading the numbers

- **Difficulty** is bikepacking.com's 1–10, blending climbing, technical difficulty,
  physical demand and resupply logistics. A route can be flat and still rate high if
  you're days from a shop.
- **% Singletrack** is the number that decides your bike. Under ~10% a gravel bike is
  fine; above that you're wanting a mountain bike or accepting some walking.
- **Drive from Portland** is my estimate to the start, not from the source.
